Wordveil Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 14, 2026

Overview

Wordveil is a word puzzle game that respects your privacy. We collect minimal, anonymous analytics data to improve the game. We do not collect, store, or share any personal information.

Data We Collect

We use TelemetryDeck, a privacy-first analytics service, to collect anonymous usage data such as:

  • Which puzzles are played and completed
  • Number of guesses per puzzle
  • Whether hints are used
  • App launch frequency

This data is fully anonymous. TelemetryDeck does not use cookies, does not collect IP addresses, and does not track users across apps or websites. No advertising identifier (IDFA) is collected.

Data We Do NOT Collect

  • Your name, email, or any contact information
  • Your location
  • Device identifiers that could identify you personally
  • The specific words or letters you type
  • Any data from other apps on your device

In-App Purchases

Wordveil offers optional in-app purchases processed entirely by Apple. We do not have access to your payment information. Purchase records are managed by your Apple ID.

Data Storage

Your game progress (completed stories, statistics, streaks) is stored locally on your device. This data never leaves your device unless you have iCloud enabled, in which case Apple may sync it across your devices using your iCloud account.

Third-Party Services

  • Apple (StoreKit) — processes in-app purchases
  • TelemetryDeck — anonymous analytics (their privacy policy)

We do not use any advertising SDKs, social media SDKs, or other third-party tracking services.

Children's Privacy

Wordveil does not knowingly collect any personal information from children. The game contains no user accounts, no social features, and no advertising.

Changes to This Policy

If we update this privacy policy, we will post the revised version on this page with an updated date.
